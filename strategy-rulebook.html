1 — SET UP THE GAME: FOLLOW THESE STEPS IN ORDER

Before anyone draws a card or takes a turn, prepare the table together.

STEP 1 · SORT ALL THE CARDS FIRST. Separate the cards by type. Do not shuffle everything together. Make the following seven separate groups:

ROLE CARDS

Separate BLUE and GREEN Role Cards into their own team groups. Each team has GOALKEEPER, PLAYER, FORWARD, DEFENDER and MIDFIELDER.

ACTION CARDS

SHOOT, DRIBBLE PAST, DEFENSE, BLOCK, TACKLE, YELLOW CARD! and COUNTERATTACK. These are the only cards shuffled into the Action Draw Pile.

MATCH CARDS

HALFTIME, SUBSTITUTION, CORNER KICK, TEAM HUDDLE and KICKOFF. Lay all five face-up as a shared reward supply.

REFEREE CARDS

IDENTITY REVEAL (working name), VAR REVIEW and PENALTY KICK. Lay all three face-up, separate from Match Cards.

GOALS AGAINST

Sort +1, +2 and +3 conceded-goal cards into their own shared supply.

GOALS SCORED

Sort +1, +2 and +3 personal scoring cards into a second shared supply. Do not mix them with GOALS AGAINST.

SOCCER

Set the single possession card aside until you choose the starting player.

STEP 2

MAKE TWO TEAMS

Split players into BLUE and GREEN teams. Establish a fixed circular turn order, alternating teams where possible.

STEP 3

DEAL ONE ROLE CARD TO EACH PLAYER

Prepare one GOALKEEPER and enough other Role Cards for each team. Deal one role to each player without revealing it to opponents. Teammates may privately share their roles. Set unused Role Cards aside.

STEP 4

SHUFFLE AND DEAL ACTION CARDS

Shuffle only the Action Cards. Deal 5 to each player. Put the remaining cards face-down in the middle as the Action Draw Pile. Leave space beside it for the face-up Action Discard Pile.

STEP 5

START ALL GOAL RECORDS AT ZERO

No player takes a GOALS AGAINST or GOALS SCORED card at setup. Leave both supplies in the middle for use during the game.

STEP 6

CHOOSE THE STARTING PLAYER

Use Rock–Paper–Scissors to determine the starting player. Give that player the SOCCER card. The player holding SOCCER has possession.

STEP 7

BEGIN THE FIRST TURN

The starting player draws one Action Card, then takes legal actions. Follow the fixed seating order after that player ends their turn.

READY TO PLAY? Each player has one Role Card and five Action Cards, the starting player holds SOCCER, both score supplies and both special-card supplies are separate, and the turn order is set.

2 — GAME OBJECTIVE & GOAL RULES

Your team wins by eliminating the opposing Goalkeeper. A Goalkeeper is eliminated after conceding 4 goals. No other role is eliminated by conceding goals.

2IF NOT REVEALED
3REVEAL ROLE
4+STAY IN GAME

After conceding 3 goals, reveal your Role Card. You remain in the game, can keep taking turns, and can continue using your Role skill. You are never eliminated by goals.

GOALKEEPER

1IF NOT REVEALED
2IF NOT REVEALED
3REVEAL ROLE
4ELIMINATED

The Goalkeeper's Role Card is revealed after conceding 3 goals. The Goalkeeper remains in the game at 3 goals and is eliminated only after conceding the 4th goal.

IDENTITY REVEAL DOES NOT MEAN ELIMINATION. A revealed player stays in the game. All players may continue taking turns and using their Role skills after their identities are public.
Track two separate personal totals: goals conceded by each player, and valid goals scored by each player. The Soccer Card only shows possession; it is not a goal counter.

3 — TAKE YOUR TURN: REPEAT THIS SEQUENCE

1DRAW 1 ACTION CARD

Draw from the top of the Action Draw Pile at the beginning of your turn.

2CHECK POSSESSION

Find out who holds SOCCER. You may shoot or pass only when you have the ball, except where a card explicitly allows otherwise.

3TAKE LEGAL ACTIONS

Play Action Cards, use your Role skill, pass, or use an earned special card at its permitted timing. Resolve each action before starting another.

4END YOUR TURN

When you are finished, say “End turn.” The next player in the fixed order starts by drawing one card, unless KICKOFF changes the order.

IF YOU HAVE SOCCER

Play SHOOT to attack one opponent, or pass the ball to a teammate for free. Passing does not give that teammate a normal turn. The Midfielder's ASSIST is the special exception that allows an immediate SHOOT.

IF YOU DO NOT HAVE SOCCER

Play TACKLE to take the ball from another player, including a teammate, or take other legal actions available to you.

YOUR TURN, YOUR CHOICE: You may take multiple legal actions after your starting draw. Active Role skills and YELLOW CARD! are each limited to once during your own turn. You may also end your turn without playing an Action Card.
Defending and counterattacking may happen during another player's turn. These reactions do not grant an extra normal turn or an extra draw. If the Action Draw Pile is empty, reshuffle discarded Action Cards to form a new draw pile.

4 — ATTACK, DEFEND AND RESOLVE THE RESULT

NORMAL ATTACK — FOLLOW EACH RESPONSE IN ORDER

SHOOT→DEFENSE→DRIBBLE PAST→BLOCK
  1. The ball holder plays SHOOT and chooses one opponent as the target.
  2. The target or a teammate may play one DEFENSE. If no DEFENSE is played, the shot scores.
  3. If DEFENSE is played, the original attacker may continue with DRIBBLE PAST. If they do not, the defense succeeds.
  4. If DRIBBLE PAST is played, the defending team may play one BLOCK. If no BLOCK is played, the shot scores. DEFENSE cannot stop DRIBBLE PAST.
  5. If BLOCK is played, the attack is stopped. The successful defender receives SOCCER.

IF THE ATTACK SCORES

SOCCER goes to the targeted player. Resolve VAR REVIEW before finalizing goal records, identity reveals, rewards or the winner.

IF THE ATTACK IS STOPPED

SOCCER goes to the successful defender. That defender may immediately play COUNTERATTACK to shoot at an opponent without using SHOOT.

COUNTERATTACK: The opposing team needs two DEFENSE cards to stop it. One player may play both, or two teammates may each play one. With zero or one DEFENSE, the counterattack scores. If two teammates stop it together, they agree which of them receives SOCCER. BLOCK cannot stop a counterattack.
Discard resolved Action Cards. After all reactions are complete, the active player's turn continues unless the game has ended or a card changes the turn order.

5 — AFTER A VALID GOAL: UPDATE BOTH PLAYERS' RECORDS

Every valid goal affects two different personal records. The shooter gains one personal goal; the target gains one conceded goal.

SHOOTER

GOALS SCORED

First goal: take +1. Second: return +1 and take +2. Third: return +2 and take +3. You may exchange each group of three valid personal goals for one available Match Card or Referee Card. If you do not exchange, keep +3 and start recording the next group separately.

TARGET

GOALS AGAINST

First conceded goal: take +1. Second: return +1 and take +2. Third: reveal your role if still hidden. The Goalkeeper takes +3; other roles return their conceded-goal cards but retain their conceded-goal history. The fourth valid goal against the Goalkeeper ends the game; no +4 card is needed.

If the needed denomination is unavailable, use equal-value combinations. For example, two +1 cards equal +2, and +2 plus +1 equals +3. Return old cards to the correct shared supply. Never mix the two score systems.
CHECK FOR THE WIN: If the current opposing Goalkeeper has conceded their fourth valid goal, your team wins immediately. Otherwise continue the current turn. Players in all other roles remain in the game.

6 — CLAIM SPECIAL CARDS AND CONTINUE PLAYING

FIRST REVEAL WITH ZERO GOALS CONCEDED

Voluntarily reveal your own Role Card, or reveal it by using your Role skill for the first time, while you have conceded zero goals. You may choose one available Match Card or Referee Card. This reward is available once per player.

EVERY THREE VALID PERSONAL GOALS

Choose whether to exchange three unspent personal goals for one available Match Card or Referee Card. Return the exchanged scoring cards. Repeat whenever you accumulate another three unspent goals. There is no fixed scoring-reward limit.

Use earned special cards according to their own timing rules. If no special cards are available, no card can be claimed. Keep playing in the fixed turn order until a Goalkeeper is eliminated.

GAME OVER: The opposing team wins when a Goalkeeper concedes their fourth valid goal. Revealing an outfield role does not eliminate that player.

8 — ROLE CARDS & ROLE SKILLS

Five roles, with one copy of each role for BLUE and one for GREEN: 10 Role Cards in total.

ROLE CARD · PASSIVE

GOALKEEPER

You are eliminated after conceding 4 goals. Reveal your Role Card after conceding 3 goals. This is a passive rule, not an active skill.

ROLE CARD · ONCE PER OWN TURN

PLAYER

Once during your turn, draw 1 additional Action Card from the Action Draw Pile. This is in addition to the normal draw at the start of your turn.

ROLE CARD · ONCE PER OWN TURN

DEFENDER

Once during your turn, exchange one DRIBBLE PAST from your hand for one DEFENSE from the discard pile.

Put the DRIBBLE PAST in the discard pile and take one DEFENSE into your hand. If no DEFENSE is available in the discard pile, you cannot use this skill. Keep the DEFENSE in your hand for later use; this is not an instant defensive reaction on another player's turn.

ROLE CARD · ONCE PER OWN TURN

FORWARD

Once during your turn, you may play one DRIBBLE PAST as SHOOT. Follow the normal SHOOT rules, including the possession requirement.

ROLE CARD · ONCE PER OWN TURN

MIDFIELDER

Once during your turn, if you hold the Soccer Card, you may pass it to a teammate, allowing them to immediately play SHOOT without waiting for their own turn.

The teammate does not draw an extra card. Resolve the attack normally, credit any valid goal to the teammate who shot, and then continue the Midfielder's turn. The normal turn order does not change.

ROLE SKILL TIMING: Each player may use their active Role skill at most once during their own turn. The allowance resets at the start of their next normal turn. Reveal your Role Card the first time you use your skill. You may continue using your skill after your identity is public. The Goalkeeper's passive elimination rule is not subject to the once-per-turn limit.
FIRST REVEAL REWARD: If you first reveal your own Role Card voluntarily or by using your Role skill while you have conceded zero goals, you may choose one available Match Card or Referee Card. This reward is available only once per player. Revealing your role after conceding a goal does not qualify.

9 — ACTION CARDS

SHOOT

On your own turn while holding SOCCER, choose an opponent and shoot. A teammate receiving the Midfielder’s ASSIST may also shoot immediately. If the opposing team plays no DEFENSE, the shot scores.

DRIBBLE PAST

After your SHOOT is defended, you may play DRIBBLE PAST to continue the same attack. Only the original attacker may play it. The defending team must play one BLOCK to stop this move; otherwise it scores. DEFENSE cannot stop DRIBBLE PAST.

The Forward may play DRIBBLE PAST as SHOOT, and the Defender may exchange it for DEFENSE from the discard pile, once per own turn.

DEFENSE

Teammates may communicate and choose who defends. One DEFENSE stops SHOOT unless the attacker continues with DRIBBLE PAST. DEFENSE cannot stop DRIBBLE PAST. A COUNTERATTACK requires two DEFENSE cards; one player may play both, or two teammates may each play one.

BLOCK

Play one BLOCK to stop an opponent’s DRIBBLE PAST after a SHOOT has been defended. The target or a teammate may play it; the team may discuss who defends. The player who successfully blocks the attack receives SOCCER. BLOCK cannot stop SHOOT or COUNTERATTACK.

TACKLE

On your own turn, take SOCCER from another player, whether an opponent or a teammate. TACKLE is not a defensive response to SHOOT.

YELLOW CARD!

Play YELLOW CARD! only during your own turn, and play at most one YELLOW CARD! per turn. Choose an opponent to restrict their actions. The exact restriction and duration are still being finalized. Do not use the old automatic next-seat skip rule.

COUNTERATTACK

After successfully defending and gaining possession, immediately play COUNTERATTACK to shoot at one opponent. No SHOOT card is required. The opposing team must play 2 DEFENSE cards to stop the counterattack. Both cards may be played by the same player, or two teammates may each play one.

Only DEFENSE can stop a COUNTERATTACK. It is a direct shot, not a normal SHOOT–DRIBBLE PAST attack chain. BLOCK cannot stop it.

COUNTERATTACK — 2 DEFENSE CARDS REQUIRED
  • 0 DEFENSE: The counterattack scores.
  • 1 DEFENSE: The counterattack still scores. The DEFENSE card played goes to the Action Discard Pile.
  • 2 DEFENSE: The counterattack is stopped. One player may play both cards, or two teammates may each play one. Both played cards go to the Action Discard Pile.
Resolve goals and possession using the normal rules. If two teammates each play one DEFENSE, they discuss and agree which of those two defenders receives SOCCER.

COUNTERATTACK & TEAM DEFENSE

A attacks. D successfully defends, gains possession, and immediately plays COUNTERATTACK. If A and teammate C each play one DEFENSE, their team stops the counterattack. If their team plays only one DEFENSE, the counterattack scores.

A: ATTACK→D: DEFENSE→D: COUNTERATTACK→A + C: 2 DEFENSE

The counterattack is not an extra normal turn and does not grant an extra draw. Once A's current turn ends, B takes the next normal turn in the fixed order.

NORMAL ATTACK FLOW

SHOOT→DEFENSE→DRIBBLE PAST→BLOCK

No DEFENSE: goal. No DRIBBLE PAST after DEFENSE: stopped. No BLOCK after DRIBBLE PAST: goal. BLOCK: stopped.

POSSESSION & DISCARD: After a valid goal, SOCCER goes to the player who conceded it. After a successful defense, SOCCER goes to the successful defender. If two teammates jointly stop a COUNTERATTACK, they agree which of them receives SOCCER. Put resolved Action Cards in the face-up discard pile. When the Action Draw Pile runs out, reshuffle discarded Action Cards only. Keep all other card categories separate.

10 — MATCH CARDS

MATCH CARD

HALFTIME

On your turn, both teams may freely redistribute Action Cards within their own team, with no exchange limit. After both teams finish, only the player who used HALFTIME draws 5 Action Cards. Possession and normal turn order stay unchanged.

MATCH CARD

SUBSTITUTION

Swap two teammates’ Role Cards, including GOALKEEPER if desired. Each player’s conceded-goal count, personal scoring records and public or hidden identity status stay with that player; they do not move with the Role Card.

MATCH CARD

CORNER KICK

Immediately after your SHOOT is stopped by a successful defense, you may play CORNER KICK to restart the attack. You must play another SHOOT, then resolve the normal attack sequence. Priority between CORNER KICK and COUNTERATTACK is still to be finalized.

MATCH CARD

TEAM HUDDLE

On your turn, only your own team may freely give, receive and exchange Action Cards among teammates. No equal trade or exchange limit is required. No extra cards are drawn; possession and turn order stay unchanged.

MATCH CARD

KICKOFF

During your turn, even after an attack has ended, choose a teammate to take the kickoff. Transfer SOCCER to that teammate regardless of its current holder. Your turn ends. All intervening seats in the fixed order are skipped, including teammates; skipped players do not draw or take normal actions. The chosen teammate starts a normal turn, draws 1, and play continues from their seat.

KICKOFF EXAMPLE · A, B, C, D, E, F

A uses KICKOFF and chooses teammate E. The Soccer Card moves to E, even if B, C, D, or F held it. B, C, and D skip their upcoming turns. E immediately draws 1 and takes the kickoff turn. After E, play continues from E's seat: F → A → B → C → D.

A→ B→ C→ D→ E ⚽→ F→ A

11 — REFEREE CARDS

There are 3 Referee Cards in total: one copy of each ability.

REFEREE CARD · 1 COPY

IDENTITY REVEAL (working name)

Choose one opponent and reveal that player’s Role Card to everyone. This does not earn that opponent a voluntary-reveal reward.

REFEREE CARD · 1 COPY

VAR REVIEW

Immediately cancel a goal just scored by an opponent. The canceled goal adds nothing to either player’s goal record. Use VAR before a third-goal identity reveal, fourth-goal elimination or scoring reward is finalized.

REFEREE CARD · 1 COPY

PENALTY KICK

Use during your own turn. Choose one opponent as the designated defender. Both players simultaneously choose LEFT, CENTER or RIGHT. Matching directions = saved; different directions = goal. No normal SHOOT or DEFENSE is required. SOCCER goes to the designated defender in either case.

12 — HOW TO EARN MATCH & REFEREE CARDS

There are two ways to earn special cards. Choose one available Match Card or Referee Card, not one of each.

REWARD 1 · IDENTITY

REVEAL YOURSELF WITH 0 GOALS CONCEDED

If you have not conceded any goals, you may voluntarily reveal your own Role Card and choose 1 available Match Card or Referee Card. Your first reveal through using your own Role skill also qualifies.

Once per player. This reward is not available if you have already conceded 1 or more goals.

REWARD 2 · SCORING

EVERY 3 PERSONAL GOALS

For every 3 valid goals you personally score, you may exchange a +3 GOALS SCORED card for 1 available Match Card or Referee Card of your choice.

Exchanging is optional. If you exchange it, return the +3 card to the shared supply. If you choose not to exchange it, keep it for later and continue recording your next goals separately.

EVERY 3 VALID PERSONAL GOALS = 1 OPTIONAL EXCHANGE. You may repeat this exchange whenever you accumulate another 3 unspent personal goals, as long as special cards are available. There is no fixed per-player limit on scoring rewards. Each group of 3 goals can be exchanged only once.
GOALS SCORED CARDS: Take a +1 card for your first valid goal. On your second goal, return +1 and take +2. On your third goal, return +2 and take +3. You may exchange +3 for a special card or keep it and begin recording the next group of goals. If a denomination is unavailable, use cards of equal total value instead (two +1 cards for +2, or +2 and +1 for +3). Return all scoring cards used in an exchange to the shared supply.
  • Match and Referee Cards are limited. Players choose from the cards still available when they claim a reward.
  • If one category is empty, choose from the other category. If both supplies are empty, no special card can be claimed.
  • Resolve VAR REVIEW before awarding scoring cards or exchanging them. Canceled goals do not count.
  • Only your own valid goals count toward your scoring exchanges, not your team's combined goals.
  • GOALS AGAINST cards cannot be exchanged for special cards.
  • An identity revealed by another player using IDENTITY REVEAL does not earn the revealed player the voluntary-reveal reward.

13 — SUBSTITUTION: ROLE SWAP EXAMPLE

When two teammates use SUBSTITUTION, they exchange Role Cards. Their personal goal records and identity status do not move.

BEFORE THE SWAP

Player A: GOALKEEPER · 0 goals conceded · identity hidden.

Player B: PLAYER · 3 goals conceded · identity revealed.

AFTER THE SWAP

Player A: PLAYER · still 0 goals conceded · still hidden.

Player B: GOALKEEPER · still 3 goals conceded · still revealed. The next goal conceded is the 4th, so Player B is eliminated.

THE RECORD STAYS WITH THE PERSON, NOT THE ROLE CARD. A revealed player remains revealed after a role swap. A player who becomes the Goalkeeper retains their own conceded-goal count. A player who becomes Goalkeeper with 3 conceded goals remains revealed, takes a +3 GOALS AGAINST card from the shared supply (or equivalent cards if needed), and will be eliminated by their next valid conceded goal. The case of a teammate who has already conceded 4 or more goals is not yet finalized.

14 — SOCCER CARD, PASS & TACKLE

SOCCER CARD = POSSESSION

The Soccer Card replaces the mini soccer piece. The player holding it has possession. It stays outside the Action deck and moves when possession changes.

PASS IS FREE

During your own turn, if you hold the Soccer Card, you may pass it to a living teammate without spending a card. The receiver gains possession but does not take an immediate turn. With the Midfielder's ASSIST skill, the receiver may immediately play SHOOT; this is an attack, not an extra normal turn.

TACKLE CAN TARGET ANY PLAYER

On your turn, use TACKLE to take the Soccer Card from any player, including someone on your own team.

KEEP TWO GOAL TOTALS

Conceded goals determine identity reveal and Goalkeeper elimination. Every 3 unspent valid personal goals may be exchanged for one available Match Card or Referee Card. Do not combine these two types of goal records.

15 — GOAL TRACKER CARDS

Use two separate sets of +1, +2 and +3 cards. Return old denominations to the shared supply whenever you exchange them for a higher denomination of equal total value. These are personal records, not Action Cards.

GOALS AGAINST

GOALS YOU CONCEDE

First valid conceded goal: take +1. Second: return +1 and take +2. Third: reveal your role if still hidden.

Goalkeeper: return +2 and take +3. A fourth valid conceded goal ends the game; no +4 card is needed.

Other roles: return your conceded-goal cards once you reach 3. Stay in the game. Remember your conceded-goal record even after returning the cards. If you later become the Goalkeeper through SUBSTITUTION with 3 conceded goals, take a +3 GOALS AGAINST card again (or equivalent cards if needed).

GOALS SCORED

GOALS YOU PERSONALLY SCORE

First valid goal: take +1. Second: exchange it for +2. Third: exchange +2 for +3. Choose whether to trade +3 for one available Match or Referee Card.

Return exchanged scoring cards to their own supply. If you keep +3, continue a new group of goals separately. Repeat for every 3 valid goals; no fixed personal reward limit.

EQUAL-VALUE EXCHANGES: If +2 is unavailable, use two +1 cards. If +3 is unavailable, use +2 and +1 or three +1 cards. Exchange them for a single denomination when it becomes available. Never mix GOALS AGAINST and GOALS SCORED. A scoring exchange consumes exactly 3 unspent personal goals.
Finalize VAR REVIEW before giving out goal records, revealing a role because of a goal, or declaring a winner. If a canceled goal was provisionally recorded, undo that change.
