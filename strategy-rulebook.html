1 — GAME OBJECTIVE & GOAL RULES

Your team wins by eliminating the opposing Goalkeeper. A Goalkeeper is eliminated after conceding 4 goals. No other role is eliminated by conceding goals.

4+STAY IN GAME

After conceding 3 goals, reveal your Role Card. You remain in the game, can keep taking turns, and can continue using your Role skill. You are never eliminated by goals.

2HIDDEN
3REVEAL ROLE
4ELIMINATED

The Goalkeeper's Role Card is revealed after conceding 3 goals. The Goalkeeper remains in the game at 3 goals and is eliminated only after conceding the 4th goal.

IDENTITY REVEAL DOES NOT MEAN ELIMINATION. A revealed player stays in the game. All players may continue taking turns and using their Role skills after their identities are public.
Track two separate personal totals: goals conceded by each player, and valid goals scored by each player. The Soccer Card only shows possession; it is not a goal counter.

3 — ROLE CARDS & ROLE SKILLS

Five roles, with one copy of each role for BLUE and one for GREEN: 10 Role Cards in total.

ROLE CARD · PASSIVE

GOALKEEPER

You are eliminated after conceding 4 goals. Reveal your Role Card after conceding 3 goals. This is a passive rule, not an active skill.

ROLE CARD · ONCE PER OWN TURN

PLAYER

Once during your turn, draw 1 additional Action Card from the Action Draw Pile. This is in addition to the normal draw at the start of your turn.

ROLE CARD · ONCE PER OWN TURN

DEFENDER

Once during your turn, exchange one DRIBBLE PAST from your hand for one DEFENSE from the discard pile.

Put the DRIBBLE PAST in the discard pile and take one DEFENSE into your hand. If no DEFENSE is available in the discard pile, you cannot use this skill. Keep the DEFENSE in your hand for later use; this is not an instant defensive reaction on another player's turn.

ROLE CARD · ONCE PER OWN TURN

FORWARD

Once during your turn, you may play one DRIBBLE PAST as SHOOT. Follow the normal SHOOT rules, including the possession requirement.

ROLE CARD · ONCE PER OWN TURN

MIDFIELDER

Once during your turn, if you hold the Soccer Card, you may pass it to a teammate, allowing them to immediately play SHOOT without waiting for their own turn.

The teammate does not draw an extra card. Resolve the attack normally, credit any valid goal to the teammate who shot, and then continue the Midfielder's turn. The normal turn order does not change.

ROLE SKILL TIMING: Each player may use their active Role skill at most once during their own turn. The allowance resets at the start of their next normal turn. Reveal your Role Card the first time you use your skill. You may continue using your skill after your identity is public. The Goalkeeper's passive elimination rule is not subject to the once-per-turn limit.
FIRST REVEAL REWARD: If you first reveal your own Role Card voluntarily or by using your Role skill while you have conceded zero goals, you may choose one available Match Card or Referee Card. This reward is available only once per player. Revealing your role after conceding a goal does not qualify.

4 — SETUP & YOUR NORMAL TURN

1MAKE TWO TEAMS

Split players into BLUE and GREEN teams. Each team has one Goalkeeper; the remaining roles are assigned from PLAYER, DEFENDER, FORWARD, and MIDFIELDER. Use the appropriate number of Role Cards for the players on each team.

2DEAL ROLE CARDS

Give each player one Role Card. Keep it hidden from opponents. Teammates may privately share their roles.

3PREPARE ACTION CARDS

Shuffle the Action Cards and deal 5 to each player. Put the remaining Action Cards face-down as the Draw Pile. Leave space for a face-up Discard Pile.

4SET UP SPECIAL CARDS

Place the Match and Referee Cards in a separate, limited reward supply. Keep the Soccer Card outside the Action deck.

EVERY NORMAL TURN STARTS WITH DRAW 1. At the beginning of your turn, draw 1 Action Card from the top of the Draw Pile before taking any action. Add it to your hand; you do not have to play the card you just drew.
  1. Draw 1 Action Card.
  2. Check who currently holds the Soccer Card.
  3. Take any legal action available to you.
  4. Resolve all responses and effects before starting another action.
  5. When finished, end your turn. Play continues in the established turn order unless a card effect changes it.
PASS IS FREE. If you hold the Soccer Card, you may pass it to a living teammate during your own turn. PASS does not require or consume an Action Card and does not give the receiving teammate an immediate turn. Exception: the Midfielder's once-per-turn ASSIST skill allows the receiving teammate to immediately play SHOOT without drawing a card or changing the turn order.

5 — ACTION CARDS

SHOOT

Starts an attack. The attacking player must have the Soccer Card and chooses an opposing player as the target.

DRIBBLE PAST

An attacking move. The Forward may play it as SHOOT once during their own turn. The Defender may exchange it for a DEFENSE from the discard pile once during their own turn.

DEFENSE

Used to defend against an opposing attack. Teammates may communicate and decide who will defend. A COUNTERATTACK requires 2 DEFENSE cards to stop it; the same player may play both cards, or two teammates may each play one.

TACKLE

Steal the Soccer Card from any player. You may tackle an opponent or a teammate.

YELLOW CARD!

The card name is confirmed. Its new effect is still being finalized; the previous automatic next-player skip rule is not carried forward here.

COUNTERATTACK

After successfully defending and gaining possession, immediately play COUNTERATTACK to shoot at one opponent. No SHOOT card is required. The opposing team must play 2 DEFENSE cards to stop the counterattack. Both cards may be played by the same player, or two teammates may each play one.

Only DEFENSE can stop a COUNTERATTACK. No DRIBBLE PAST is needed, and BLOCK cannot stop it.

COUNTERATTACK — 2 DEFENSE CARDS REQUIRED
  • 0 DEFENSE: The counterattack scores.
  • 1 DEFENSE: The counterattack still scores. The DEFENSE card played goes to the Action Discard Pile.
  • 2 DEFENSE: The counterattack is stopped. One player may play both cards, or two teammates may each play one. Both played cards go to the Action Discard Pile.
Resolve any goal and possession changes using the normal rules.

COUNTERATTACK & TEAM DEFENSE

A attacks. D successfully defends, gains possession, and immediately plays COUNTERATTACK. If A and teammate C each play one DEFENSE, their team stops the counterattack. If their team plays only one DEFENSE, the counterattack scores.

A: ATTACK→D: DEFENSE→D: COUNTERATTACK→A + C: 2 DEFENSE

The counterattack is not an extra normal turn and does not grant an extra draw. Once A's current turn ends, B takes the next normal turn in the fixed order.

The exact timing, limits, and card interactions for SHOOT, DRIBBLE PAST, DEFENSE, and YELLOW CARD! will be finalized in the next Action Card review.

6 — MATCH CARDS

MATCH CARD

HALFTIME

Both teams may exchange cards freely within their own team. After all team exchanges are complete, the player who used HALFTIME draws 5 Action Cards.

MATCH CARD

SUBSTITUTION

Swap Role Cards with a teammate. Each player's conceded-goal count and identity-reveal status stay with that player; they do not move with the Role Card.

MATCH CARD

CORNER KICK

After a SHOOT is successfully stopped by DEFENSE, play CORNER KICK to earn a corner. Restart the attack with SHOOT. The attack can still use DRIBBLE PAST, and the defending team can still play DEFENSE. Resolve the new attack through the normal attack process.

MATCH CARD

TEAM HUDDLE

Your team may freely give, receive, and exchange cards among teammates. There is no exchange limit, no required equal trade, and no card-count restriction.

MATCH CARD

KICKOFF

Choose a teammate to take the kickoff. Give them the Soccer Card even if another player currently holds it. The chosen teammate immediately starts a normal turn and draws 1 Action Card first. The turn order then continues from the kicker's seat.

KICKOFF EXAMPLE · A, B, C, D, E, F

A uses KICKOFF and chooses teammate E. The Soccer Card moves to E, even if B, C, D, or F held it. B, C, and D skip their upcoming turns. E immediately draws 1 and takes the kickoff turn. After E, play continues from E's seat: F → A → B → C → D.

A→ B→ C→ D→ E ⚽→ F→ A

7 — REFEREE CARDS

There are 3 Referee Cards in total: one copy of each ability.

REFEREE CARD · 1 COPY

IDENTITY REVEAL (working name)

Choose one player and reveal that player's Role Card to everyone.

REFEREE CARD · 1 COPY

VAR REVIEW

Cancel a goal that was just scored if it is ruled invalid. Remove that goal from the scorer's personal goal total and from the target's conceded-goal total. If it was the 3rd conceded goal, VAR must be used before the Role Card is revealed. VAR may also cancel a Goalkeeper's 4th goal, but it must be used before the game ends.

REFEREE CARD · 1 COPY

PENALTY KICK

Use during your own turn. Choose one opponent as the designated defender. The shooter and defender each choose LEFT, CENTER, or RIGHT. If they choose the same direction, the shot fails and the Soccer Card goes to the designated defender. If they choose different directions, the shot scores.

8 — HOW TO EARN MATCH & REFEREE CARDS

Match and Referee Cards are limited special rewards. When you meet a reward condition, choose 1 available card from either category.

REWARD 1 · IDENTITY

REVEAL YOURSELF WITH 0 GOALS CONCEDED

If you have not conceded any goals, you may voluntarily reveal your own Role Card and choose 1 available Match Card or Referee Card. Your first reveal through using your own Role skill also qualifies.

Once per player. This reward is not available if you have already conceded 1 or more goals.

REWARD 2 · SCORING

REACH 3 PERSONAL GOALS

When you personally score your 3rd valid goal, choose 1 available Match Card or Referee Card.

Once per player at this milestone.

REWARD 3 · SCORING

REACH 6 PERSONAL GOALS

When you personally score your 6th valid goal, choose 1 more available Match Card or Referee Card.

Once per player at this milestone.

MAXIMUM: 3 SPECIAL CARDS PER PLAYER. One for revealing your identity with 0 goals conceded, one at 3 personal goals, and one at 6 personal goals.
  • Special cards are limited. Players who qualify earlier choose first.
  • If all Match Cards are gone, choose from the remaining Referee Cards. If all Referee Cards are gone, choose from the remaining Match Cards.
  • If both supplies are empty when you qualify, no special card can be claimed.
  • A goal canceled by VAR REVIEW does not count toward either player's goal totals or reward milestones.
  • An identity revealed by another player using IDENTITY REVEAL does not earn the revealed player the voluntary-reveal reward.

9 — SUBSTITUTION: ROLE SWAP EXAMPLE

When two teammates use SUBSTITUTION, they exchange Role Cards. Their personal goal records and identity status do not move.

BEFORE THE SWAP

Player A: GOALKEEPER · 0 goals conceded · identity hidden.

Player B: PLAYER · 3 goals conceded · identity revealed.

AFTER THE SWAP

Player A: PLAYER · still 0 goals conceded · still hidden.

Player B: GOALKEEPER · still 3 goals conceded · still revealed. The next goal conceded is the 4th, so Player B is eliminated.

THE RECORD STAYS WITH THE PERSON, NOT THE ROLE CARD. A revealed player remains revealed after a role swap. A player who becomes the Goalkeeper uses their own existing conceded-goal total to determine whether they have reached the 4-goal elimination threshold.

10 — SOCCER CARD, PASS & TACKLE

SOCCER CARD = POSSESSION

The Soccer Card replaces the mini soccer piece. The player holding it has possession. It stays outside the Action deck and moves when possession changes.

PASS IS FREE

During your own turn, if you hold the Soccer Card, you may pass it to a living teammate without spending a card. The receiver gains possession but does not take an immediate turn. With the Midfielder's ASSIST skill, the receiver may immediately play SHOOT; this is an attack, not an extra normal turn.

TACKLE CAN TARGET ANY PLAYER

On your turn, use TACKLE to take the Soccer Card from any player, including someone on your own team.

KEEP TWO GOAL TOTALS

Conceded goals determine identity reveal and Goalkeeper elimination. Personal goals scored determine special rewards at 3 and 6 goals. Do not combine these totals.
