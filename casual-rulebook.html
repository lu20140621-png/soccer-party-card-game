PLAY CARDS. DRAW LAST.

Bluff, reverse, reflect and spring traps on your friends. Draw SOCCER twice without a save and you're out. Be the last player standing.

1 — SET UP THE GAME IN THIS EXACT ORDER

1

SORT ALL THE CARDS FIRST

Separate the seven card groups. Do not shuffle all the cards together yet.

SHUFFLE FIRST

ACTION CARDS · 64

SHOOT, DRIBBLE PAST, DEFENSE, BLOCK, TACKLE, COUNTERATTACK and YELLOW CARD!

ADD AFTER DEALING

MATCH CARDS · 5

HALFTIME, SUBSTITUTION, CORNER KICK, TEAM HUDDLE and KICKOFF.

ADD AFTER DEALING

REFEREE CARDS · 3

IDENTITY REVEAL (working name), VAR REVIEW and PENALTY KICK.

TRAPS · ADD AFTER DEALING

GOALS SCORED · 13

+1 ×8, +2 ×3, +3 ×2. These are playable trap cards, not scoring records in this mode.

KEEP SEPARATE

GOALS AGAINST · 13

+1 ×8, +2 ×3, +3 ×2. Use +1 and +2 to track conceded goals. Set +3 aside.

KEEP SEPARATE FIRST

SOCCER · 1

One danger card. Do not deal it in the starting hands.

SHUFFLE SEPARATELY

ROLE CARDS · 10

Shuffle all 10 Role Cards together, regardless of team color. Each player randomly draws one Role Card. Set unused Role Cards aside. Each player may actively use their role ability once per game.

2

CHOOSE PLAYERS AND TURN ORDER

Play with 2–8 players, everyone for themselves. Choose a starting player and begin in clockwise order. All goal records start at zero.

3

SHUFFLE ACTION CARDS AND DEAL FIVE

Shuffle only Action Cards. Deal exactly 5 to each player. Keep hands hidden. Set the remaining Action Cards in the middle.

4

ADD THE NEW CARDS AFTER DEALING

Shuffle all 5 Match Cards, all 3 Referee Cards and all 13 GOALS SCORED trap cards into the remaining Action Cards. Insert the single SOCCER card into the resulting Draw Pile at position 2 or deeper, as in the original setup. HALFTIME and KICKOFF can later put SOCCER on top.

5

PREPARE THE TABLE

Put the Draw Pile face-down. Prepare two separate face-up discard piles: one for Action Cards and GOALS SCORED trap cards, and one for Match and Referee Cards. Keep GOALS AGAINST in a separate shared supply. Shuffle all 10 Role Cards together and let each player randomly draw one. Set unused Role Cards aside.

6

START PLAYING

The starting player may play cards first. Do not draw at the beginning of the turn.

READY TO PLAY: Everyone has 5 Action Cards and one Role Card; the remaining Draw Pile contains Action, Match, Referee, trap and SOCCER cards. There are 86 cards in the draw/hand system before dealing, using the current provisional 64-Action-Card configuration.

2 — YOUR TURN — PLAY FIRST, DRAW LAST

1

PLAY CARDS — OPTIONAL

Play as many legal cards as you want, one at a time. You may also actively use your once-per-game Role ability at its permitted timing. Resolve each effect and any reactions before starting the next one. You may play no cards.

2

DRAW ONE CARD

When finished, take the top card of the Draw Pile. Resolve SOCCER immediately. Other drawn cards enter your hand, including Match, Referee and GOALS SCORED trap cards.

3

END YOUR TURN

Once your own final draw and all resulting effects are resolved, play passes to the next active player in the current direction. Skip eliminated players.

FORCED DRAW ≠ TURN END: A draw caused by SHOOT or another effect does not end the active player's turn. If the active player is eliminated, their turn ends after the current effect is resolved.
BLOCK CHANGES THE DIRECTION: The active player continues their current turn. The next turn and trap-chain direction follow the new direction of play.

3 — SOCCER — TWO CONCEDED GOALS AND YOU ARE OUT

Whenever you draw SOCCER, stop and resolve it immediately. SOCCER never stays in your hand.

SAVE YOURSELF

DEFENSE

Only the player who drew SOCCER may play DEFENSE to prevent the conceded goal. VAR REVIEW or an unused GOALKEEPER Role ability may also prevent an ordinary conceded goal. None of these can prevent a PENALTY KICK result.

FIRST CONCEDED GOAL

GOALS AGAINST +1

Take a +1 record card. Stay in the game.

SECOND CONCEDED GOAL

GOALS AGAINST +2

Return +1 and take +2 if available. You are immediately eliminated. If +2 is unavailable, two +1 cards record the same total.

RETURN SOCCER: After an ordinary SOCCER draw is resolved, the player who drew it chooses any position except the top of the Draw Pile, even if eliminated. HALFTIME, KICKOFF and the MIDFIELDER Role ability allow SOCCER to be on top. There is only one SOCCER card.
WIN: The last active player wins. No team play or personal goal-scoring rewards in Casual Party Mode.

4 — ACTION CARDS — ALL SEVEN ABILITIES

FORCE A DRAW

SHOOT

Choose yourself or another active player. The chosen player immediately draws one card from the top of the Draw Pile after responses are resolved.

PEEK AT THREE

DRIBBLE PAST

Privately look at the top 3 cards. Return them in the exact same order. Do not rearrange them.

SAVE YOURSELF

DEFENSE

When you draw SOCCER, play DEFENSE to prevent that ordinary conceded goal. Only the player drawing SOCCER may use it. YELLOW CARD! cannot cancel DEFENSE.

REVERSE

BLOCK

Reverse the direction of play immediately. Clockwise becomes counterclockwise, or vice versa. Continue the current turn.

STEAL A CARD

TACKLE

Choose another active player with at least one hand card. Take one random card from their hand and add it to yours.

REFLECT SHOOT

COUNTERATTACK

When another player uses SHOOT on you, play COUNTERATTACK before the forced draw. That SHOOT is reflected back to the player who played it: they draw one card instead of you. Only the original target may use COUNTERATTACK; you cannot reflect a SHOOT aimed at someone else.

CANCEL AN ACTION

YELLOW CARD!

Cancel a pending Action Card effect before it resolves. This includes SHOOT, DRIBBLE PAST, BLOCK, TACKLE, COUNTERATTACK or another YELLOW CARD!. It cannot cancel DEFENSE, SOCCER, Match Cards, Referee Cards, trap cards or role abilities.

YELLOW CARD! CHAIN: Any player may respond before the pending effect resolves. A second YELLOW CARD! can cancel the first, allowing the original action to resolve. Resolve the chain from the newest response backward. A card drawn after an action starts resolving cannot retroactively cancel it.
COUNTERATTACK EXAMPLE: A plays SHOOT on B. B plays COUNTERATTACK. A draws one card; B does not draw. A's normal turn continues after the effect resolves.

5 — MATCH CARDS — ALL FIVE ABILITIES

SHUFFLE THE ENTIRE DRAW PILE

HALFTIME

Shuffle the entire Draw Pile, including SOCCER. SOCCER may end up in any position, including the top.

SWAP HANDS

SUBSTITUTION

Choose another active player. Exchange your entire hands. Do not exchange Role Cards or GOALS AGAINST records.

THREE RANDOM CARDS

CORNER KICK

Take the top 3 cards of the Draw Pile, shuffle those three cards face-down, and give one to each of three chosen players. Recipients resolve SOCCER immediately if they receive it; other cards enter their hands.

SEARCH AND SWAP

TEAM HUDDLE

Look through the Draw Pile. Choose one Action Card and exchange it for one Action Card from your hand. Then shuffle the Draw Pile, including SOCCER.

REPOSITION SOCCER

KICKOFF

Find SOCCER in the Draw Pile and place it back in any position you choose, including the very top.

IMPORTANT: Match Cards are not Action Cards and cannot be canceled by YELLOW CARD!. After use, place them in the separate Match & Referee Discard Pile. Never shuffle this pile back into the Draw Pile.

6 — REFEREE CARDS — ALL THREE ABILITIES

LOOK AT A HAND

IDENTITY REVEAL

Choose one opponent and privately look at their entire hand. Return all cards to that player.

CANCEL AN ORDINARY CONCEDED GOAL

VAR REVIEW

When you are about to concede a goal, immediately play VAR REVIEW to prevent that ordinary conceded goal. It cannot prevent a PENALTY KICK result.

UNCANCELABLE PENALTY

PENALTY KICK

Choose an opponent. Take SOCCER and two other cards from the Draw Pile. Shuffle these three cards and lay them face-down in LEFT, CENTER and RIGHT positions. Your opponent chooses one. If they choose SOCCER, they concede one goal. Otherwise they are safe. No card can cancel or prevent this penalty or its result, including DEFENSE, VAR REVIEW and YELLOW CARD!.

7 — GOALS SCORED — TRAP CARDS

These cards do not record goals in this mode. Their numbers are random-discard penalties.

TRAP

+1 · 8 COPIES

Randomly discard 1 hand card if you must take the penalty.

TRAP

+2 · 3 COPIES

Randomly discard 2 hand cards if you must take the penalty.

TRAP

+3 · 2 COPIES

Randomly discard 3 hand cards if you must take the penalty.

1

START A TRAP CHAIN

During your turn, play a GOALS SCORED trap card. The next active player in the current direction must respond or accept the penalty.

2

PASS THE PENALTY ON

The next player may play one trap card to pass the penalty to the following active player. Proposed playtest rule: different values can stack, and their numbers are added together.

3

TAKE THE PENALTY

A player who does not respond randomly discards the required number of hand cards. If they have fewer, discard all their hand cards. This does not itself count as a conceded goal.

EXAMPLE: A plays +1, B responds with +1, C responds with +2. Under the proposed stacking rule, D must randomly discard four cards if D does not respond.
TRAP TIMING: Trap cards are held in hand until played. YELLOW CARD! cannot cancel them. Proposed details: a player may choose to accept the penalty, one trap per response, the chain does not change the normal turn order, and used traps go to the Action & Trap Discard Pile. Any discarded special cards stay separate.

8 — ROLE CARDS — ONE ACTIVE ABILITY PER GAME

Shuffle all 10 Role Cards together and randomly draw one per player. No teams. Two players may receive the same role; each has their own single use. All five Role abilities are active abilities. Choose when to activate them according to the timing below. Each may be used only once per game.

ONCE PER GAME

GOALKEEPER

When you draw SOCCER, you may prevent the conceded goal without playing DEFENSE or discarding any hand cards. Cannot prevent a PENALTY KICK result.

ONCE PER GAME

PLAYER

During your own turn, choose any two Action Cards from the Action & Trap Discard Pile and add them to your hand. You cannot take GOALS SCORED traps or Match or Referee Cards.

ONCE PER GAME

FORWARD

During your own turn, choose any active player, including yourself, to immediately draw three cards, one at a time. Fully resolve each draw before the next. No SHOOT card is required.

ONCE PER GAME

DEFENDER

When another player targets you with an Action Card or a GOALS SCORED trap, actively use this ability before the effect resolves to reflect the attack back to the player who used it. The attacker receives the effect instead of you. Cannot reflect Match or Referee Cards.

ONCE PER GAME

MIDFIELDER

During your own turn, look through the Draw Pile, find SOCCER and move it to any position you choose, including the top. Keep all other cards in their existing order.

MARK THE USED ROLE: Suggested table procedure: keep your Role Card hidden until you activate its ability, then reveal it and turn it sideways to show that it has been used. All role abilities leave the normal turn order unchanged. PENALTY KICK cannot be canceled by any role ability.
DEFENDER EXAMPLE: A plays TACKLE on B. B activates DEFENDER. B takes one random hand card from A instead of A taking a card from B. For a single trap, A plays +2 on the next player B; B activates DEFENDER, so A receives the two-card random-discard penalty instead. The exact reflection target and accumulated penalty in a longer trap chain are still to be finalized.

9 — DISCARD, REFILL AND WIN

1

DISCARD RESOLVED CARDS

Put resolved Action Cards and GOALS SCORED trap cards in the face-up Action & Trap Discard Pile. Put used Match Cards and Referee Cards in a separate face-up Match & Referee Discard Pile. Never mix these piles. Used Match and Referee Cards cannot be recycled or recovered.

2

REFILL THE DRAW PILE

When the Draw Pile runs out, shuffle only the Action & Trap Discard Pile to create a new Draw Pile. Match and Referee Cards in the separate discard pile never return. If an effect needs three cards and fewer are available, the suggested refill procedure is to place the remaining Draw Pile cards on top of the newly shuffled recyclable cards without changing their order. When returning SOCCER to an empty pile after an ordinary draw, refill first if possible. Keep GOALS AGAINST and Role Cards out of the Draw Pile.

3

ELIMINATION

On the second conceded goal, stop taking turns and no longer receive new attacks or traps. Discard your remaining hand cards into their respective piles (proposed procedure for unused Match and Referee Cards: place them in the non-recyclable special-card pile). Finish resolving the current effect before moving on. Return SOCCER if it was drawn normally.

4

LAST PLAYER WINS

Continue in the current direction, skipping eliminated players. The last active player is the winner.

10 — A COMPLETE FIRST-TURN EXAMPLE

1

A LOOKS AT THEIR FIVE CARDS

A does not draw yet.

2

A PLAYS DRIBBLE PAST

A privately sees the top three cards and puts them back in order.

3

A PLAYS SHOOT ON B

B plays COUNTERATTACK, reflecting the forced draw back to A.

4

A DRAWS ONE

A resolves the forced draw. If still active, A can continue playing cards.

5

A PLAYS BLOCK

The direction of play reverses. A's turn is still active.

6

A FINISHES AND DRAWS ONE

Resolve the final draw. The next active player in the new direction starts their turn.

11 — REMAINING PLAYTEST DETAILS

Confirmed card abilities above are separated from these implementation decisions that still need final approval.

  • Role Card visibility and sideways marking are suggested table procedures; the five abilities and once-per-game limit are confirmed.
  • DEFENDER reflection in a multi-player trap chain: which attacker receives the accumulated penalty and whether any additional reflection is allowed.
  • PLAYER ability when fewer than two Action Cards are in the recyclable discard pile; FORWARD if the target is eliminated before completing three draws.
  • Whether mixed +1/+2/+3 trap values stack, and exact response timing.
  • CORNER KICK with only two active players: proposed option is to allow a recipient to receive more than one card, resolving cards one by one.
  • PENALTY KICK return procedure after selection.
  • Used Match and Referee Cards are permanently non-recyclable. Handling unused special cards discarded by traps or elimination and the exact early-refill procedure remain to be finalized.
  • How reflected SHOOT interacts with another COUNTERATTACK or DEFENDER. Suggested test rule: reflect only once per original SHOOT.
  • Final Action Card quantities after balance testing.
